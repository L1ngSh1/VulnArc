# Responsible Disclosure

VulnArc research is limited to authorized projects and local environments. Never test third-party production systems without explicit authorization, and follow each upstream project's security policy.

Undisclosed hypotheses, reproduction evidence, embargoed findings, and private reports belong only in a separate local/private workspace and never in this public repository. Report findings privately before publication. Publish reproduction information only when coordinated disclosure permits it, after sanitization and review.

VulnArc has no automatic disclosure, publishing, upload, commit, or push behavior. The researcher remains responsible for authorization, validation, coordination, and publication decisions.
